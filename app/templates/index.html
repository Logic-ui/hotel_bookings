Strategic Hospitality Takeaways

  • City Hotels suffer ~14% higher cancellation rates than Resort properties due to transient business travelers and flexible online bookings.
  • Lead Time is the strongest cancellation predictor: Reservations made over 180 days in advance have cancellation rates exceeding 55%.
  • Special Requests signal guest loyalty: Customers requesting 2 or more special accommodations cancel 60% less frequently.
  • The "Deposit Paradox": Non-refundable deposits in this dataset were largely applied to high-risk group tour agency blocks, resulting in high recorded cancellations.
